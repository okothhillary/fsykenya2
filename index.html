Install it, don't use the browser

We strongly recommend installing the app on your home screen instead of opening it in the browser. It opens faster, stays signed in, and works full screen like a normal app.

Install in Chrome (Android)

  1. If you had already installed it, remove the old icon: press and hold FSY Check-in on your home screen, then tap Uninstall or Remove..
  2. The below steps are for everyone, whether you have installed the app or not: Tap Open the new app above and sign in with your Google account.
  3. Tap Open the new app above and sign in with your Google account.
  4. Tap the three dots ⋮ at the top right of Chrome.
  5. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  6. If asked, choose Install, then tap Install or Add again to confirm.
  7. From now on, open FSY Check-in from the new icon on your home screen.

Other phones

  1. Brave: tap the three dots ⋮, then Install and create shortcut.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
